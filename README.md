#  HARRY STYLES: *Dance All the Time. Retro, Occasionally.* 🕺🏻👾

I signed up for Hacknotts 2026 but unfortunately couldn't attend in person. Rather than letting the opportunity go to waste, I decided to take on the challenge from home and build my own game inspired by the retro 2000s theme.

This is my take on the challenge, combining my love for music with my desire to improve my coding skills and explore game development. **No LLMs were used to write the code for this project.**

## ✨ Inspiration

Building on my previous music-related projects, I wanted to turn my experience of seeing Harry Styles perform at Wembley into a retro video game.

The idea is to recreate the atmosphere of that wonderful night through a pixel-art concert scene, featuring a tiny dancing character inspired by Harry and rhythm-based gameplay. Players will follow falling arrows and press the corresponding keys to make the character dance along to the beat.

It's my way of capturing a memorable concert experience in a nostalgic, early-2000s game style while challenging myself to build something independently.
